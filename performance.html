02

“How do I get each of them to their best?”

The manager

A quarterly check-in built from the outcomes: what the evidence shows so far, and one focus for next quarter. Strong performers get stretch at their level, or toward the next one if they want it. Best at their level is a good place to be.

  • Quarterly check-in
  • Development focus

03

“Someone's slipping. What do I do?”

The manager

Act early, and be specific. A Focused Development Plan names the one outcome that's off track, the support on offer, and what good looks like in six weeks. It starts from evidence the engineer has already seen in check-ins, so nothing comes as a surprise.

  • Focused Development Plan
  • Weekly check-ins

back on track in six weeks
04

“It isn't improving. Is this fair?”

The manager and HR

If the plan hadn't worked, the next step is a performance improvement plan (PIP). It uses the same outcomes the person was hired against, the evidence from every check-in, and support written in. Nothing in it is new to them, and HR can check that.

  • Performance improvement plan (PIP)
  • HR review

If it hadn't worked.

Performance improvement plan (PIP), excerpt

  • Built onThe same outcome, the same evidence. Nothing new.
  • SupportA named mentor, weekly check-ins, clear targets
  • HR checksWas it raised early? Was help offered? Is the bar the same for everyone?
  • Ends inBack on track, a role that fits better, or a fair exit. All of it on the record.
most people never get here
05

“Are we judging everyone the same way?”

HR

In review season, HR lines up every manager's ratings against the same outcomes. “Strong” in one team means the same as “strong” in another, and the discussion is about evidence, not who argues best.

  • Review cycle
  • Calibration

the same bar in every team

When goals change mid-year, the review changes with them.

In month six the bank raises the cost target from 20% to 30%. Change it once, and the next check-in, the team view and the year-end review form all update, so nobody is judged on a goal that no longer applies. Try it.
