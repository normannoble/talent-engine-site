Job ad

We're looking for a DevOps rockstar who thrives in a fast-paced environment and loves new tech!

Weakness?

Interview guide

Tell me about yourself. What is your greatest weakness? Where do you see yourself in five years?

Tell me about a service level you set. What happened when it slipped?

Walk me through the worst incident you led, minute by minute.

When did a cloud bill last surprise you? What did you change?

Laptop?

Onboarding plan

Week 1: collect laptop, HR induction, read the team wiki. Then get stuck in.

Days 31–60: agree service levels for the three most critical journeys.

Turn on cost tagging; send the first spend report by team, against the 30% target.

Days 61–90: close one audit finding with a platform control.
