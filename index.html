Four documents.
Four different jobs.

Most companies write the job ad, the interview, the onboarding and the promotion criteria at different times, by different people. So each one describes a different job, and nine months in nobody can say whether the hire worked.

sound familiar?
Rockstar?Job ad

We're looking for a DevOps rockstar who thrives in a fast-paced environment!

Weakness?Interview

Tell me about yourself. What is your greatest weakness?

Then what?Onboarding

Week 1: collect laptop, read the wiki. Then get stuck in.

Time served?Promotion

Eligible after two years in role, on manager recommendation.

02

Every interviewer tests something different.

The interview panel

Each stage owns one or two outcomes. Nobody asks the same question twice, every score rests on evidence, and the debrief only discusses the disagreements.

  • Guide per stage
  • Scorecard
  • Debrief sheet
teratai / senior-platform-engineer / interview-plan

Who tests what.

Four stages, five outcomes, no overlap

  • Hiring manager"Tell me about a service level you set. What happened when it slipped?"
  • Technical exerciseDesign the path from commit to production for a new service.
  • Risk and security"Show us audit evidence your platform produced on its own."
  • Final"Walk me through the worst incident you led, minute by minute."
Each interviewer scores alone, then the panel compares evidence.
no question asked twice
