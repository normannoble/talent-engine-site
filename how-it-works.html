Opinionated by design.

Generating one good job description is easy. Keeping every level and every artifact consistent, across a whole team and as roles change, is not. Three things make it work.

  • ◆

    An opinionated architecture

    Outcomes at the root, and every artifact traceable back to them. It's what keeps hundreds of artifacts coherent, and it's the part that can't be improvised.

  • ✦

    AI for the repetitive work

    Drafting consistent artifacts across every level and every stage, in days rather than months.

  • ●

    People for the judgement

    Norman shapes every engagement. Your HR validates every artifact before it goes live.

the architecture is the secret sauce
